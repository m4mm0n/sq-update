# Privacy Policy for Sweet Quake - Linux

**Last updated: July 9, 2026**

ZeroLinez Softworx operates **Sweet Quake** for Linux desktop systems. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Linux version of the game.

## Developer and contact

**Developer:** ZeroLinez Softworx  
**Game:** Sweet Quake  
**Platform:** Linux desktop  
**Country:** Norway  
**Privacy and support:** zls@darkmaster.no  
**Administrative contact:** admin@darkmaster.no

## Information that may be processed

Sweet Quake may process:

- Local game progress, unlocked levels, scores, achievements, coins, boosters, settings, and save data.
- Technical information such as game version, Linux distribution or desktop environment, device type, language, display settings, crash details, and performance diagnostics.
- Optional account or online-service information if cloud saves, scoreboards, Discord links, Facebook connection, tester features, or similar online features are enabled.
- Support information you send to us, including your email address and message contents.

Please do not send sensitive personal information in support messages unless it is needed to resolve your request.

## Purchases on Linux

Direct Linux builds may show an optional external PayPal booster shop and a redeem-code field. Opening the shop leaves the game and opens a ZeroLinez Softworx web page. PayPal handles checkout and payment information. A completed order can issue a redeem code, which the player enters in Sweet Quake to claim the listed in-game reward. Sweet Quake and the ZeroLinez Softworx service may process the code, stable local player identifier or connected Facebook identifier, reward, redemption status, and transaction reference needed for support, fraud prevention, and one-time fulfillment. ZeroLinez Softworx does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

## Local data

Game settings, save data, scores, and progress may be stored locally on your Linux device. Local data can usually be removed by deleting the game's local configuration/save folder or uninstalling the game. Data synchronized through optional online services may need a separate deletion request.

## How information is used

Information may be used to:

- Run and maintain Sweet Quake.
- Save and restore progress, settings, achievements, and scores.
- Issue, validate, and fulfill optional external-shop redeem codes.
- Provide optional cloud-save, leaderboard, tester, support, or community features.
- Diagnose crashes, errors, performance issues, and compatibility problems.
- Prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and security incidents.
- Improve gameplay, balancing, reliability, accessibility, and usability.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Third-party services

Depending on enabled features, Sweet Quake may use PayPal for optional external checkout; Cloudflare-hosted ZeroLinez Softworx services for scoreboards, redeem codes, cloud saves, tester features, and public legal pages; Facebook/Meta login or profile services; Discord community links or webhooks; and email provider services. Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

## Advertising

The Linux desktop build does not use Google Play mobile advertising surfaces. If advertising is added to a future Linux build, the release notes, store listing if any, and this policy will be updated before release.

## Children and family safety

Sweet Quake is designed to be family friendly. We do not knowingly request personal information from children directly. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us and we will investigate and delete it where required.

## Data retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and technical operation. Information no longer required will be deleted, anonymized, or securely isolated unless retention is legally required.

## Data deletion

You may request deletion of personal information controlled by ZeroLinez Softworx by emailing **zls@darkmaster.no** with the subject **Sweet Quake Data Deletion Request**. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant data without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

Norwegian users may contact Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

## Changes

We may update this policy when Sweet Quake, platform requirements, services, or data-handling practices change. The page will show an updated date.
