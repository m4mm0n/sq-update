Privacy Policy for Sweet Quake - Windows Store

Last updated: July 9, 2026

ZeroLinez Softworx operates Sweet Quake for Windows Store. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Windows Store version of the game.

Developer and Contact

Developer: ZeroLinez Softworx
Game: Sweet Quake
Platform: Windows Store / Windows desktop
Country: Norway
Privacy and support: zls@darkmaster.no
Administrative contact: admin@darkmaster.no

Information That May Be Processed

  • Local game progress, unlocked levels, scores, achievements, coins, boosters, settings, and save data.
  • Technical information such as game version, Windows version, device type, language, display settings, crash details, and performance diagnostics.
  • Optional account or online-service information if cloud saves, scoreboards, Discord links, Facebook connection, tester features, or similar online features are enabled.
  • Support information you send to us, including your email address and message contents.

Please do not send sensitive personal information in support messages unless it is needed to resolve your request.

Purchases on Windows Store

The Windows Store build is prepared as a Store-specific release. External PayPal and Google Play purchase surfaces are removed from this build. If paid content is added for Windows Store later, it is planned to use the Microsoft Store commerce system or another Store-compliant flow and this policy will be updated before release.

Local Data

Game settings, save data, scores, and progress may be stored locally on your Windows device. Local data can usually be removed by uninstalling the game or deleting its local application data. Data synchronized through optional online services may need a separate deletion request.

How Information Is Used

  • Run and maintain Sweet Quake.
  • Save and restore progress, settings, achievements, and scores.
  • Provide optional cloud-save, leaderboard, tester, support, or community features.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, performance issues, and compatibility problems.
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Improve gameplay, balancing, reliability, accessibility, and usability.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

Depending on enabled features, Sweet Quake may use Microsoft Store platform services, Cloudflare-hosted ZeroLinez Softworx services, Facebook/Meta login, Discord community links or webhooks, and email provider services. Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising

The Windows Store build should not use Google Play mobile advertising surfaces. If advertising is added to a future Windows Store build, the store listing and this policy will be updated before release, and any required consent or child-safety controls will be used.

Children and Family Safety

Sweet Quake is designed to be family friendly. We do not knowingly request personal information from children directly. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us and we will investigate and delete it where required.

Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and technical operation. Information no longer required will be deleted, anonymized, or securely isolated unless retention is legally required.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information controlled by ZeroLinez Softworx by emailing zls@darkmaster.no with the subject Sweet Quake Data Deletion Request. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant data without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Norwegian users may contact Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We may update this policy when Sweet Quake, platform requirements, services, or data-handling practices change. The page will show an updated date.
