Sweet Quake Account and Data Deletion

ZeroLinez Softworx — Last updated June 22, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information associated with Sweet Quake. If a future version adds a full account system, this page will be updated with the in-game deletion flow.

Request deletion by email

Send an email with the subject Sweet Quake Account Deletion Request to zls@darkmaster.no.

Include your player name, approximate install date, device type, and any other information needed to identify your data. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Information deleted

  • Account profile information controlled by ZeroLinez Softworx.
  • Server-side game progress and settings associated with the account.
  • Other personal information associated with the account, unless retention is legally required.

Information that may be retained

Limited information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, purchase and accounting records, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or enforcement of applicable terms. Retained information will be restricted to those purposes and kept only for the required period.

Local data

Data stored only on your device can normally be removed by clearing Sweet Quake’s application data or uninstalling the game.
