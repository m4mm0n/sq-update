2. Information that may be processed

The information processed depends on the features and third-party services enabled in the version of Sweet Quake that you use.

Information you provide

  • Your email address and the contents of messages sent to customer support.
  • A chosen player name, profile name, or account information, where account features are available.
  • Information supplied when reporting a problem, joining a closed test, or requesting assistance.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a support message unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Game, app, and device information

Sweet Quake or services used by the game may process information such as:

  • Device type, model, operating-system version, language, and regional settings.
  • Game version, installation identifier, and technical configuration.
  • Gameplay progress, level results, achievements, feature usage, and settings.
  • Crash reports, error logs, diagnostic data, and performance information.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers, where advertising is enabled and legally permitted.
  • Information necessary to detect fraud, cheating, abuse, or technical problems.

Purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, payment processing is handled by Google Play. ZeroLinez Softworx may receive the product purchased, purchase status, transaction or purchase identifier, purchase time, and information needed to verify or restore the purchase. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

3. Local game data

Game settings, unlocked levels, scores, progress, and save data may be stored locally on your device. Locally stored information may be removed when you clear the game’s application data or uninstall the game, unless it has also been synchronized through an account or cloud-save service.

4. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • Operate, maintain, and provide Sweet Quake.
  • Save and restore progress, settings, achievements, or purchases.
  • Process, verify, and restore in-app purchases.
  • Provide leaderboards, achievements, cloud saves, or account features.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported problems.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and performance problems.
  • Improve gameplay, balancing, reliability, performance, and usability.
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Display advertising, where advertising is enabled.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or similar law applies, information may be processed on one or more of these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: when processing is necessary to provide the game or a feature you request.
  • Legitimate interests: including security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, and reasonable product improvement.
  • Consent: when consent is required for advertising, analytics, optional permissions, or similar processing.
  • Legal obligation: when information must be retained or disclosed under applicable law.

6. Advertising

Sweet Quake may display advertisements supplied by third-party advertising providers. Depending on your location, age, consent choices, and device settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information. Where required, consent will be requested before information is used for personalized advertising. Android privacy settings may allow you to reset or restrict the use of your advertising identifier.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

We may use analytics and crash-reporting services to understand how Sweet Quake performs and to identify technical problems. These services may process app usage and gameplay events, app version, device information, installation identifiers, crash logs, stack traces, performance information, IP address, and approximate location.

8. Third-party services

Sweet Quake may use third-party services that process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob

Third-party providers may act as independent controllers or as processors providing services to ZeroLinez Softworx. We do not sell personal information.

9. Information sharing

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with:

  • Hosting, cloud-storage, infrastructure, and technical-service providers.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers.
  • Advertising providers, where advertising is enabled.
  • Payment and purchase-verification providers.
  • Security, fraud-prevention, and customer-support providers.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law.
  • A successor organisation in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the game, subject to appropriate safeguards.

10. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Norway or the European Economic Area. Where required, international transfers are protected using recognised legal safeguards, such as adequacy decisions, contractual protections, or standard contractual clauses.

11. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. Appropriate encrypted connections are used for network transmission where available. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Retention may depend on how long information is needed to provide a feature, security and fraud-prevention requirements, support and dispute-resolution needs, legal obligations, financial record requirements, and the retention settings of third-party services.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted, anonymised, or securely isolated unless continued retention is legally required.

13. Data and account deletion

You may request deletion of personal information controlled by ZeroLinez Softworx by contacting: zls@darkmaster.no. Use the subject Sweet Quake Data Deletion Request and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, transaction records, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Data stored only on your device can normally be removed by clearing Sweet Quake’s application data or uninstalling the game.

Account deletion webpage: Account and data deletion instructions.

14. Children’s privacy

Sweet Quake is not directed specifically at children under the minimum digital-consent age applicable in their country. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so that we can investigate and delete the information where required.

If Sweet Quake is offered to children in a particular country or version, child-directed advertising, data processing, consent, and SDK requirements applicable to that audience will be followed.

15. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to receive information about processing.
  • The right to access and correct personal information.
  • The right to request deletion or restriction of processing.
  • The right to object to certain processing.
  • The right to data portability where applicable.
  • The right to withdraw consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

Norwegian users may lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

16. Permissions

Sweet Quake requests Android permissions only when required for an available feature. Where required, the reason for sensitive access will be explained at or before the permission request. You may revoke granted permissions through Android settings, although the relevant feature may then stop working.

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sweet Quake, its services, legal requirements, or data-handling practices change. The revised policy will show an updated “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in the game, through the store listing, or by another appropriate method.
